The chart screen should feel like a personal identity card, beautiful enough to screenshot and share.

Reading tone should feel like **a wise elder**

Every screen, even negative readings, must end on a growth-oriented note. Visual design should use warm, calming colors, not anxiety-inducing dark themes.
